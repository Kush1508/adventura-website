Is registry immediate upon full payment?

Yes. Dream City Phase 4 is fully documented under Official Registration No: 00340. Once your payment terms are completed, immediate registry with Dakhil-Kharij assistance is executed.

What road widths are provided in the townships?

Our townships feature 30 ft. wide main boulevards, 20 ft. secondary roads, and 16 ft. internal paved lanes to ensure smooth vehicle movement and emergency access.

Adventura provides direct in-house flexi-installments for qualifying plots without complicated bank mortgage paperwork, allowing investors to secure plots with predictable monthly outflows.
